What this app does

MyBlaCa is a dedicated notebook for patients with urothelial carcinoma — a place to keep track of your diagnosis, cystoscopies, surgeries and bladder instillations between hospital visits.

It is not a tool for making treatment decisions. It does not replace your urologist — it simply helps you keep the facts straight for yourself and for your appointments.

Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no login, and no cloud server — your data never leaves your phone unless you choose to export a backup file yourself.

Install it on your phone

MyBlaCa installs like a regular app — with its own icon on your home screen — straight from your browser, in under a minute. No app store, no account.

  1. Open the app link in ChromeUse the Chrome browser on your phone (not another browser) and open the MyBlaCa link your clinic or family member gave you.
  2. Open the menuTap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"Chrome may show this automatically as a banner at the bottom of the screen — you can tap that instead.
  4. ConfirmTap "Add" or "Install". The MyBlaCa icon now appears on your home screen like any other app.
  5. Open it from the iconFrom now on, always open MyBlaCa from its home screen icon — it works offline and keeps your data safely between visits.

Why it has to be added this way: MyBlaCa isn't in the App Store or Google Play — it runs directly from the web, so "Add to Home Screen" is what turns it into an app icon on your phone. This also means there is nothing to buy, no account to register, and no company holding your medical data.

Using the app day to day

  1. Fill in Primary data onceYour diagnosis details, from your pathology report or your doctor. You can always come back and edit it.
  2. Add an event after each visitA cystoscopy, a urine test, an instillation, or a surgery — takes under a minute to log.
  3. Check History and Current status anytimeSee your full timeline, filter by type, and view your recommended follow-up schedule.
  4. Back up occasionallyFrom the About screen, export a backup file — especially before you change phones — and import it on your new device to carry your history over.

Common questions

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything you enter is stored only in your phone's browser storage. There is no server, no account and no cloud sync of any kind.

What happens if I get a new phone?

Export a backup file from the About screen on your old phone (e.g. by emailing it to yourself or saving it to cloud storage you use), install MyBlaCa on the new phone, and import that file from its About screen.

Can I print my records for an appointment?

Yes — open Current status and use the print/save-as-PDF button, or print the History table.

Does this replace talking to my doctor?

No. MyBlaCa is a personal notebook, not a diagnostic or decision-making tool. Always discuss your results and follow-up plan with your urologist.
